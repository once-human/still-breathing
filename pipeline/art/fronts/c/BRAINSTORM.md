# 01 crowd: front brainstorm

The back is the tour crowd from behind, one ink-line drawing: cream line (#F1E7D0) with yellow (#F6B330), green
(#56B27C) and blue (#4C66F5) spot inks on the black tee. Bengaluru on the left (the green line pulling in at
Madavara, rain trees, an auto on Tumakuru Road), Mumbai on the right (the skyline behind the race course, the twin
crowns at Tardeo, a crane, the course rails, a plane lining up for BOM, a December moon), the stage and its beams in
the middle, and the people: a girl in a yellow kurta on her friend's shoulders, a yellow monkey cap with both arms up,
a tall guy with his phone torch, mallige in a braid, two friends hugging, a steel tumbler of filter coffee. Notes in
a hand ("on her friend's shoulders", "monkey caps out", "best friends since 7th std"), again.. and india . 12 . 2026
above.

So the front should never be a second crowd poster. It should be the other side of that night: what the stage saw,
one person from it up close, an object in someone's hand, the sky over it, a footnote. Same pen, same four inks, so
front and back read as one shirt. The back's lines are about 1.2–1.5 mm wide at print; the fronts keep that weight
(never under 1 mm), which is why the small pieces have to be drawn simply and boldly, not as shrunk crowds.

Who buys it: the people at both nights. Many are not from the city; they love the night, the lights, the friends they
went with. Things they would recognise from their own night, not local insider motifs.

## Ideas (by placement)

**Full front**
1. *What the stage sees.* The same crowd from the other side: faces lit, eyes shut, mouths open on the drop, hands up,
   phones, the girl on shoulders now facing us. The back's reverse shot. The beams come from behind the viewer and
   land on them.
2. *The block of light (linocut).* The stage glow as one cream block; the crowd's heads and hands carved out of it as
   black silhouettes against it. The beams carved as gouges. The whole night as one graphic shape.
3. *The PA hang.* A real line array hanging from the roof truss, its curve, the rigging, one tech on the deck. Too
   gear-nerd for this crowd: rejected.

**Centre chest**
4. *She turned round.* The girl on her friend's shoulders (from the back) turning round to look at you over her
   shoulder, laughing, arms still up, the braid swinging, yellow kurta, blue bangles. Her friend's head and hands on
   her shins under her. One person, big and drawn properly.
5. *Last train out.* The moment after: inside the green line's last train from Madavara, people asleep on each other,
   shoes in hand, a wristband. Lovely but a third scene with many people; it fights the back. Kept as a reserve.
6. *Hands against the light (linocut, smaller).* Idea 2 as a chest-size block, a ragged oval of light with five hands
   and two heads carved out of its bottom edge.

**Left chest**
7. *Filming it.* A hand holding a phone up, the stage tiny on its screen with its beams, the rec dot on. The one thing
   everyone at the show did. Small, bold, readable.
8. *Filter coffee, still hot.* The steel tumbler and dabara from the back, steam rising. Charming but a food icon on
   a concert shirt reads as a café logo. Rejected.
9. *The monkey cap.* The yellow monkey cap with its bobble, alone. Too much of an in-joke without the scene.

**Low hem**
10. *Linked.* Two friends' hands linked, held up, one with a friendship band, one with bangles, coming up from the hem
    corner the way they come up out of the crowd. "best friends since 7th std", drawn rather than written.
11. *The front row.* A row of hands and heads rising from the hem across the whole width, as if the wearer were the
    stage. Good, but overlaps with 1; folded into it.

**Vertical strip**
12. *One beam.* A single white stage beam falling down one side of the chest from the collarbone to the ribs, drawn as
    broken dry-brush strokes like the beams on the back, and at its foot a few hands catching it.
13. *The green line.* The viaduct climbing the strip, a train on it, Madavara at the top. Reads as a metro diagram:
    the owner already threw one out ("AI-ish"). Rejected.

**Collarbone**
14. *A december moon.* The back's note, lifted out and written large by hand, with its arrow pointing to the crescent
    moon and the plane lining up for BOM under it. The sky above both nights.
15. *The twins.* The two crowned towers at Tardeo with the crane, small, just under the neck. Pretty, but an icon on
    its own; it belongs in 16.

**Split**
16. *Two nights.* The Mumbai skyline (the twin crowns, the crane, the course rails) on the chest, and low on the other
    side the green line train pulling into Madavara at the hem. One shirt, two cities, the same night, answering each
    other diagonally across the body.
17. *Phone and screen.* A small phone on the chest and its footage (the stage) at the hem. Clever on paper, gimmicky
    on a body. Rejected.

**Lettering**
18. *again.. on a wristband.* The four scripts woven into a fabric event wristband round a raised wrist. Too close to
    merch. Rejected.
19. *again.. as the old block.* Not allowed by the brief and not wanted.

Why these: seven placements, from a 9.5 cm mark to a full front; four media (the back's ink line, dry brush, hand
lettering, linocut); seven of eight wordless and none uses again.., because the back already carries again.. large
and the old front was all again..; the one with words is a real note from the back. Each pairs with the back in its
own way: the reverse shot (02), one person lifted out (01), an object from it (03), its two cities pulled apart (04),
its beams (05), its friendships (06), its sky and its notes (07), its light as one shape (08). Each also has to stand
alone for someone who only sees the front.
