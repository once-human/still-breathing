# 01 crowd: fronts, the brainstorm

## What the front has to sit with

The back is the loudest thing in the lineup: a full ink-line drawing of the crowd from behind, hands and phones up,
the stage with its marigold beams, the Mumbai towers on one side and the Green Line at Madavara on the other, cream
line with loose green, marigold and blue screens, on black. Anything busy on the front fights it. So the front is
the quiet side: small, mostly the chest, mostly type, one idea each, in the back's own inks (cream #F1E7D0, marigold
#F6B330, green #56B27C, blue #4C66F5) and its own hand (a wobbling, pressing pen; loose spot colour off register).

Rules taken from his notes: never the back's picture as the subject; a wordmark in nearly every one (again..,
fred again.., actual life, again.. in four scripts); no slogans, tracklists or clip-art; the graphic alone on black.

## Ideas (16)

1. **Filming, quiet.** The liked idea, emptied out: one cream line for a phone held up by a thumb and two fingers,
   the screen a flat green, and on it one marigold dot, the stage light, the whole night reduced to one light.
   again.. under it, its two dots marigold to answer the light.
2. **Viewfinder.** fred again.. framed by a camera's four corner brackets, a marigold rec dot and a seven-segment
   timecode. Filming again, as interface. (Too close to 1, and too much like an app screen.)
3. **Two nights.** again, set big, with its two dots made the subject: one marigold, one green, the two nights of
   the back. A hand note under each dot: mumbai, bengaluru.
4. **Four scripts on one line.** again.. / फिर से.. / पुन्हा.. / ಮತ್ತೆ.. in one long line along the collarbone,
   small, each pair of dots in another of the back's inks. The old stacked block undone into a sentence.
5. **The wristband.** The woven festival band everyone wore both nights, laid flat in a slow curve, marigold, with
   again.. again.. again.. woven along it and its plastic slide and cut tail at the ends.
6. **Gaffer tape.** A strip of cream gaffer tape, torn at both ends, actual life written on it in marker, the way a
   setlist or a mark is taped to a stage floor.
7. **The entry stamp.** The rubber stamp put on your wrist at the gate: an oval in blue, fred again.. around the
   rim, two dots in the middle, the ring broken where the stamp didn't press.
8. **The rig.** fred again.. set solid in cream with a fan of marigold beams rising from behind it, the word as the
   stage. Seven strokes, nothing else.
9. **Holding on.** Two hands clasped, drawn in one cream line, the crowd shirt's real subject (people from both
   cities together) without drawing the crowd; again.. under it.
10. **Crescent.** A small marigold moon over actual life. (Pretty but generic; the back already has its moon.)
11. **The camcorder date.** A seven-segment orange date stamp, 12 2026, next to again.., like a frame of home
    video. (A gimmick on its own; folded into nothing.)
12. **Lanyard pass.** An all-areas laminate: fred again.. / crowd. (Merch cliché.)
13. **Hands up as the dots.** again with its two dots replaced by two tiny raised fists. (Clip-art.)
14. **Torch field.** A grid of small lit phone rectangles at different heights, one reading again... (That is the
    back's crowd again, abstracted. Out by his first rule.)
15. **Tally of nights.** Two tally marks and again... (Reads as a prison wall.)
16. **One more.** The chant for an encore as lettering. (A slogan. Out.)

## The eight, and why

| no | name | wordmark | where | why it earns a place |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | filming | again.. | left chest 9.5 cm | his liked idea, kept and emptied: one object, one light, one word |
| 02 | holding on | again.. | left chest 10 cm | the people of the back as one gesture, no crowd drawn |
| 03 | two nights | again.. | centre chest 14 cm | pure wordmark; the dots carry the shirt's two nights |
| 04 | one line | four scripts | collarbone 24 cm | the four scripts as a sentence, not a block; the quietest one |
| 05 | wristband | again.. ×3 | centre chest 20 cm | a real object of the night that holds the word naturally |
| 06 | tape | actual life | left chest 10 cm | stage floor, marker hand, the album name where it belongs |
| 07 | stamp | fred again.. | left chest 9 cm | the gate's stamp as a badge, one ink, one shape |
| 08 | the rig | fred again.. | centre chest 20 cm | the boldest; the word is the stage, the back's beams answer it |

Each is a different kind of thing: a drawn object (01, 02), pure lettering (03, 04), a real artefact of the night
carrying the word (05, 06, 07) and an emblem (08). Every one carries a wordmark. Viewfinder (2) lost to 01 as the
filming idea; the rest lost for being clichés, slogans, generic, or the back's picture again.

## Method

art-agent, the light loop: a half-page brief per version in its notes.md, three draft rounds of render, look, fix
(drafts at 4.7 px/mm, crops at print size), one fresh critic over all eight, one rework, then the final at 94 px/cm,
cleaned with an octagon opening (1 mm strokes survive at any angle) and checked with check.py verify.
Lettering is set from the lineup's fonts (Bricolage Grotesque for again.., the Noto scripts, Gochi Hand for the
back's note hand) and then worked by hand: each glyph nudged off the baseline and turned a hair, the edges spread
like ink on cotton, and the hand-held parts (marker, stamp, weave) drawn with the pen.

## Change: two fronts, not eight

The owner then asked for two fronts, the artist's pick, made as well as possible. The first round of eight drafts
went to one critic. Its read: the drawn objects (hands especially) and the font-made handwriting were the weak
point, filming was the most on-brand idea, and the wordmark drawn in the back's own technique tied best to the back.
The two picked:

- **01 filming**: the liked idea, taken further. The screen is dark like the night, the stage small and lit, and the
  hand drawn properly.
- **02 drawn** (was 08): fred again.. drawn in the back's pen and loose spot screens; new, and the closest tie to
  the back without using its picture.

The other six (holding on, two nights, one line, wristband, tape, stamp) were dropped. Two nights and one line were
clean but light; the rest failed in the drawing or read as clip-art.
