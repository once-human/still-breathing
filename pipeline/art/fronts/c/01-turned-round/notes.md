# 01 turned round

**Placement:** centre chest, 27 cm wide, top 10 cm under the shoulder point. **Inks:** the back's cream line, yellow,
blue, green. **Words:** none.

**The idea.** On the back, a girl in a yellow kurta sits on her friend's shoulders with her arms up, seen from behind.
Here she has turned round to face you: laughing with her eyes shut, one arm high, the other thrown out wide, her braid
swung forward over her shoulder with its blue paranda, jhumkas swinging, bangles on both wrists. Under her, her friend
in a green tee holds her shins and looks up at her, laughing too. One person lifted out of the crowd and drawn big.

**How it pairs.** The same people and the same pen as the back, the same four inks. Whoever looks at the back finds her
from behind on the right of the crowd; whoever looks at the front gets her face. It stands alone as two friends at a
show without needing the back.

**Critic pass (one).** Acted on: the near-mirrored pose (now one arm high, one wide, the head tilted), a bigger laugh
with teeth and tongue, a real friend underneath (tilted head, eyes up at her, open laugh, green tee, hands round her
shins), the stamped dot row at the hem replaced by two drawn border lines and a drape over his head, heavier fine
lines, printed at 27 cm instead of 24. Not acted on: making it 30 cm tall plus "ground" — centre chest stays under
28 cm wide, and a ground line would box it in.

**Weak spots.** The kurta is still one big flat yellow shape; the braid's links are regular; his forearms are plain
tubes running out of the bottom.
