# 02 drawn: centre chest, about 21 cm

**Brief.** fred again.. drawn the way the back is drawn. Every letter's outline is traced from the lineup's face
(Bricolage, heaviest weight) and redrawn with the back's cream pen, steady with a little press and lift. The back's
spot screens sit under it loose and off register: each letter's screen slips up or down by about 1.3 mm of visible
colour, a different amount per letter. Like the crowd on the back, the colours wander across the word: green under
f, d, the g; marigold under e, both a's, n; r and i left bare. The first dot is a ring with the blue screen slipped
under it; the last is solid cream. Under the right half, small and set in the same face: mumbai · bengaluru ·
december 2026.

**With the back.** It takes the back's hand (cream line, loose green / marigold / blue screens, misregistration),
never its picture. On the chest it reads as the brand's wordmark at arm's length; up close it reads as drawn.

**Placement.** Centre chest, 20.9 cm wide, top about 11 cm under the high shoulder point.

**Process.** Six draw, look, fix passes (the letter tracer built from scratch, the g closed into one form, the slip
made a clear 1.3 mm so no sub-mm slivers remain); one fresh critic; one rework (colours wander across the word, each
screen slips its own way up or down, two bare letters, blue on one dot, a tighter word gap, the second line added).

**Weak spots (honest).**
- The letters are traced from a font, then drawn over; they are not lettered from scratch, so the shapes are the
  face's, not a hand's. The critic asked for true hand lettering; that would be the next step.
- The g is closed into a solid form with two counters so it reads cleanly; it is a little heavier than the other
  letters.
- A couple of small green bits sit in the d's counter; they pass the 1 mm check, but only just.
