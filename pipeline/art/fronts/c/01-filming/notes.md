# 01 filming: left chest, about 8 cm

**Brief.** What everyone at the show did, drawn once and properly. A hand holds a phone up, screen toward us. The
screen is dark like the night it films. On it the drop, as a phone sees it from far back: a small lit stage (a
marigold deck, its LED wall the one sliver of green), six marigold beams fanning up off it, and a row of heads in
front drawn as small cream rounds on a shoulder line. A blue rec dot and a cream timer tick sit top left. The hand is
drawn properly: four fingers curled round the right edge (tapering, the little finger short and tucked), a thumb on
the glass with its nail, and the palm's edge running down into the forearm. The forearm wears a marigold thread and a
blue band. The drawing stops partway down the arm, and again.. sits beside it with its two dots in the beams' marigold.

**With the back.** The same cream pen and loose spot screens (green, marigold, blue) as the back's crowd, but one
object and one word. It takes the owner's liked phone idea and drops the busy, fully drawn stage of the first round.
The front is the phone in one person's hand; the back is everyone.

**Placement.** Left chest, 8.0 cm wide, top about 15 cm under the high shoulder point.

**Process.** Seven draw, look, fix passes; one fresh critic; one rework (dark screen instead of a full green one;
heads as cream rounds; tapering fingers; bands wrapping the wrist; forearm ticks gone; word pulled in closer).

**Weak spots (honest).**
- again.. is set from the lineup face (Bricolage), not drawn by hand. That keeps the brand mark exact, but it is
  plainer than the drawing beside it.
- The leftmost beam bends where it meets the screen's edge.
- The top right of the piece is open; at 8 cm on the chest that reads as air, not as a gap.
