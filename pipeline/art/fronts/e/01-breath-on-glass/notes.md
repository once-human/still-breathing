# 01 breath on glass

**Idea.** The cab home after the show. The bottom corner of the window has fogged with our breath, and someone writes
again.. in it with a fingertip, the line drifting up as the cab moves. The fog is one flat cream shape tucked into the
window's corner: its left and bottom edges follow the rubber seal with a strip of clear glass between, its top and right
edges are free where the breath thinned out. The word is wiped out of it, so the shirt's black is the night through the
letters. Each stroke is one steady fingertip width with a fatter pad where the finger first pressed. One drip runs from
the n and ends in a bead. Behind the glass a sodium streetlamp sits under the dots: the first dot is all orange, the
second is cut by the lamp's edge, half orange and half night. That split is what puts the light behind the glass.

**Placement.** Left chest, about 12.5 cm wide, top about 15 cm under the high shoulder point.

**With the back.** The back is the heat inside the show; this is the cold outside it, the same breath on glass on the
way home. Cream and the back's orange, nothing from its picture.

**How it got here.** Three early rounds, then eight more. A halftone edge on the fog (read as splatter) and a lamp
drawn outside the fog with rings of light (read as an icon) were tried and taken out. One fresh critic said the centred
oval read as a speech bubble and the seal as a graph axis. In the rework the fog moved into the seal's corner, the seal
became one steady line with a round corner, the lettering went to one finger width with pads and a drift, the lamp
became one hidden disc splitting the second dot, and the drip tapers to a bead.

**Weak spots.** The fog's edge is a hard cut (DTG can't print a soft one), so it leans on the seal and the drip to read
as fog. The fog's top-left is a square corner. The dots are small next to the letters.
