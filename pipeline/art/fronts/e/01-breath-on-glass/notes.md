# 01 breath on glass

**Idea.** The cab home after the show. The window has fogged with everyone's breath, and someone writes again.. on it
with a fingertip. One flat cream fog shape, the word wiped out of it so the shirt's black is the night through the
letters, one drip from the n. The two dots are orange: a streetlamp behind the glass, seen only where the dots were
wiped. The window's rubber seal is one cream line under the fog that turns up into the rounded corner and stops.

**Placement.** Left chest, about 12 cm wide, top about 15 cm under the high shoulder point.

**With the back.** The back is the heat inside the show; this is the cold outside it, the same breath on glass on the
way home. Cream and the back's orange, nothing from its picture.

**Weak spots.** The fog's edge is a hard, cut edge (DTG can't print a soft one). Without the drip and the seal it could
read as a plain oval patch. A halftone edge was tried and dropped (it read as splatter). The fresh critic pass was
started but not finished (usage limit), so the one rework after it has not been done yet.
