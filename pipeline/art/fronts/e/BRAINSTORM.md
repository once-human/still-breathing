# 02 i am a party: fronts, the brainstorm

**The back:** both nights seen by a heat camera from the stage. Hands up for the drop, breath rising into the December
air, the crowd in banded thermal colour (deep violet, magenta, red, orange, amber, pale cream), handwritten notes:
"bengaluru, 16° at night", "mumbai, 20° at night", "the warmest place in the city", "can't feel my nose, don't care",
"hands up for the drop", "his first fred show". Lettering: Gaegu Bold cream, the date in brand blue, notes in Gochi Hand.

**The danger:** he called a heat-camera crowd "AI-ish". The back's look comes from smooth blurred contours. The front
must carry warmth, breath and body heat **by hand**: every band is its own hand-cut shape (a separate screen, drawn,
not computed from a blur), nested like riso separations or a reduction linocut, a little off register. No smooth
gradient, no camera interface (no scale bar, no crosshair, no °C readout, no frame).

**The facts:** December nights. Bengaluru lows about 15–16°, Mumbai about 19–20° (the back's numbers). Delhi NCR,
5 December, about 9–10° at night. Body surface about 33–35°, breath leaves the mouth near 34° and cools in a hand's
length. On a heat camera: hands, faces, necks and armpits glow; noses, ears, fingertips, hair and jacket sleeves read
cool; breath shows as a warm plume that cools as it rises; a handprint left on cold metal glows and fades.

**The inks (reused from the back):** violet `#5B1F8E`, magenta `#A3217E`, red `#DC3D4B`, orange `#F2802A`, amber
`#F7C24D`, cream `#F6EFD2`, brand blue `#4C66F5`. Each version uses at most four, light on the black shirt.

## Ideas (by placement)

**Left chest (8–11 cm)**
1. **One hand.** A single raised hand from the stage's side, palm out, fingers loose. Hand-cut heat bands: palm core
   cream, fingers cooling to red at the tips, the jacket cuff violet. Palm creases carved out like a linocut.
2. **again.., written by a warm finger.** The word as a heat trail a finger leaves on cold metal: the first letter
   already cooled to violet, the dots still cream-hot. Time is in the colour; it says "go again".
3. **A handprint on the barricade.** The front row pressed their hands on the steel; the camera sees the print
   glowing, then fading. Small, strange, true.
4. **The cold nose.** A face from the front, eyes shut, all warm except the violet tip of the nose.

**Collarbone (12–20 cm)**
5. **Breath off the collar.** A chain of breath puffs leaving the neckline, drifting up and out over the shoulder,
   warm (amber) where it leaves the mouth, cooling to violet and breaking up as it rises.
6. **16° / 20°** as two small drawn breaths, the Bengaluru one bigger (colder air, more visible breath), the Mumbai
   one smaller, each with its number in the notes' hand.

**Centre chest (20–28 cm)**
7. **The warmest place in the city.** Two friends hugging at the drop: two heads and four arms, their heat merging
   into one cream core where cheek meets cheek and arm meets back.
8. **A bouquet of hands.** Nine hands up, packed tight, from the stage. The back's crowd distilled to its gesture.
9. **Two cities, one high five.** Two hands meeting; one cooler band set (16°), one warmer (20°). Too neat: cut.

**Full front (up to 35 × 45 cm)**
10. **Hands up for the drop.** A field of raised hands rising up the whole front, big and hot near the bottom, small
    and few toward the chest, the cold dark between them. The back's crowd with everything but the hands taken away.
11. **His first show.** One big face, head tipped back, eyes shut, mouth open, breath leaving it: a heat portrait cut
    in a few flat bands like a reduction linocut.

**Low hem (10–30 cm)**
12. **The drop.** One strip of people all in the air at once, feet off the ground, arms flung up, their heat a row of
    bright shapes over the cold ground line.
13. **The city outside.** The cold edge of the night at the hem in one cool ink (trees, a water tank, a lamp), with
    the crowd's warmth breaking in at one end.

**Vertical strip (5–8 cm × up to 40 cm)**
14. **Head back.** A single dancer stretched from fingertips to hips, arms straight up, head thrown back, breath
    rising off the mouth above the hands. A tall thin figure that fits a strip exactly.
15. **A column of breath** rising up the side from hem to chest. Too close to 5: cut.

**Split (two pieces)**
16. **Can't feel my nose / don't care.** The cold-nose face on the chest, and down at the hem, under it, the note in
    the back's hand, like a footnote. Mark and caption, a metre of joke stretched over the body.
17. **Hand and print.** A raised hand on the chest and its handprint on the barricade at the hem. Weaker than 1 + 3.
18. **The heat camera's legend as a hand-cut paper strip** (16° to 37°). That is a camera interface: cut.
19. **Shoes off the ground** at a hem corner, the drop seen from the feet. Funny, but less than 12.

## The eight, and why

| # | name | placement | size | medium | words |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | one hand | left chest | 9.5 cm | reduction linocut: four hand-cut bands, carved creases | none |
| 02 | breath | collarbone (wearer's right) | 17 cm | riso: nested puff shapes, one screen per band | none |
| 03 | head back | vertical strip (wearer's right) | 7 × 38 cm | cut-paper bands, one figure | none |
| 04 | can't feel my nose | split: left chest + hem | 9 cm + note | ink line + spot heat fills, the note in the back's hand | the note |
| 05 | hands up for the drop | full front | 34 × 40 cm | screen print, four inks, thinning to the edges | none |
| 06 | the drop | low hem (wearer's left) | 26 cm | flat silhouettes in bands over a hand ground line | none |
| 07 | the warmest place | centre chest | 25 cm | reduction linocut, big shapes | none |
| 08 | again.. | left chest | 10 cm | one continuous heat stroke, nested bands | again.. |

Why these: they cover seven placements, from a 9.5 cm mark to a full front, and three densities (one mark, a figure,
a field). Six carry no words; again.. appears once, and not as the old block but as the heat a finger leaves, which
only this shirt could say. Each lifts one thing out of the back: its gesture (01, 05), its breath (02), its dancer
(03), a note (04), its moment (06), its warmth between people (07), its time (08). Each also stands alone: a hand, a
breath, a hug read without the back. The camera itself never appears; only what it sees, cut by hand.
