# 02 i am a party: fronts, the brainstorm (third round)

**What the back is.** Both nights through a heat camera from the stage: a packed crowd, hands up for the drop, in
banded thermal colour (deep violet, magenta, red, orange, amber, pale cream), the title "i am a party" in the cream
hand, the date in brand blue, notes in the small hand: "bengaluru, 16° at night", "mumbai, 20° at night", "the warmest
place in the city", "can't feel my nose. don't care". It is the loudest back of the four.

**What the front must be.** Calm. The back is a wall of heat, so the front is mostly the black shirt with a small,
carefully made wordmark on it, and at most one small idea of warmth or breath. Never the back's picture (no hands, no
crowd, no faces, no thermal scene), never a smooth gradient, never anything that looks like a camera's interface (no
scale bar, no crosshair, no °C readout, no frame corners). He called that kind of thing AI-ish.

**What the first round got wrong here.** It drew a raised hand and breath rising off the collar in heat bands: both
pieces of the back's picture. All of that is gone. Nothing below is a picture from the back.

**Inks** (from the back, light on the black shirt): cream `#F1E7D0` (the back's lettering), pale yellow `#FFC04E`,
orange `#F27C24`, violet `#6B2499` (the back's violet `#541278`, a step lighter so it holds on the cloth). Brand blue
`#4C66F5` is there for the dots if a version wants the brand's own mark. At most three inks in any one.

**The hand.** The Latin wordmarks are drawn letter by letter with the toolkit's pen (a monoline brush with pressure,
wobble and overshoot), the way the back's cream lettering is hand-drawn. Where the four scripts appear they are set
from the lineup's fonts (Bricolage Grotesque, Noto Devanagari, Noto Kannada, as the current front sets them) and then
cut by hand.

## Ideas

1. **A warm word.** again.. cut like a reduction linocut in three nested bands: a cream core, an orange band, a violet
   rim, each band its own hand-cut screen a little off register. The back's heat lives in the letters, nowhere else.
2. **Two nights.** again.. in cream, its two dots drawn as two small warm spots (pale yellow, orange) with a note in
   the small hand under each: bengaluru 16°, mumbai 20°. The punctuation is the tour.
3. **Breath on glass.** again.. written with a finger through a patch of breath fog on a cold window (the cab home,
   the bus): a pale, hand-cut fog shape with the word wiped out of it, so the shirt's black is the letters. One drip.
4. **Four scripts on one line.** again.. फिर से.. पुन्हा.. ಮತ್ತೆ.. in a single line along the collarbone, cream,
   the dots warm. The brand's four scripts, but never the old stacked block.
5. **The seal.** A round seal: the four scripts running round a hand-drawn ring, a small warm core at its centre cut
   in three nested shapes (violet, orange, pale yellow). The emblem of the tour.
6. **Quiet violet.** fred again.. drawn large across the chest in the back's violet only, tone on tone on black, the
   two dots orange. The most restrained: no picture at all, a wordmark you notice up close.
7. **Rising.** fred again at the lower hem, small; its two dots lift off the line and rise as two breaths, getting
   bigger and fainter-drawn (an open, broken line) as they go.
8. **Warm arcs.** actual life in cream, three broken arcs of warmth over it (violet, orange, pale yellow), like heat
   leaving a body or sound leaving a stack. A cousin of the Mumbai half circles he liked.
9. **Breath bubble.** actual life written inside an exhale's outline, like a speech bubble made of breath. Too cute:
   cut.
10. **A ticket stub** with fred again.. and the date. Generic merch: cut.
11. **A woollen scarf stripe** in the back's bands at the collarbone. A pattern, not an idea, and gimmicky: cut.
12. **The December moon** over again.., the cold outside the warm crowd. Pleasant but has nothing to do with this
    shirt more than any other: cut.
13. **A degree sign.** A huge hand-drawn ° with again.. inside it. Too clever, reads as a weather logo: cut.
14. **The warmest place in the city** as a lettered line along the hem. A slogan from the back's notes: cut.
15. **A handprint on cold steel** (the barricade). The back's hands again, in another pose: cut.
16. **A thermometer** or temperature scale under the word. A camera/weather interface: cut.
