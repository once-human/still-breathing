# 02 warm word

**Idea.** fred again.. drawn by hand in the back's cream, with two hand-drawn contour lines round it: orange close in,
violet further out, black between. They sit a little higher than the letters, wider apart above than below, so the
warmth rises off the name. Between the letters they run together into one outline. The counters stay dark.

**Placement.** Centre chest, about 22 cm wide, top about 11 cm under the high shoulder point.

**With the back.** It carries the back's heat bands in the lettering alone: no crowd, no gradient, no camera interface.

**Weak spots.** At thumbnail size the two lines buzz a little. The rising effect is subtle. The double outline is close
to a 1970s outlined-type look. The fresh critic pass was started but not finished (usage limit), so the one rework
after it has not been done yet.
