# 02 warm word

**Idea.** fred again.. drawn by hand in the back's cream, with the warmth it gives off drawn as two isotherms. An
orange line rises off the tops of the letters, in soft plumes over the f, the d and the i where the strokes reach up.
A violet line goes round the whole word as one slow outline, well off above (heat rises) and close underneath. The
further out, the smoother: the orange still knows the letters, the violet doesn't. Counters stay dark, nothing is
filled, no gradient, no camera interface.

**Placement.** Centre chest, about 17.5 cm wide, top about 11 cm under the high shoulder point.

**With the back.** It carries the back's heat in two lines round the name: the crowd's warmth, with no crowd.

**How it got here.** It started as solid nested bands (read as 1970s bubble type), then as two contour lines offset
from the letters. One fresh critic said those read as a sticker outline: every line copied every wobble, the rise was
invisible, and there were specks. In the rework the lines come from a rising heat field round the letters, so they get
smoother the further out they are. The orange keeps only the warmth going up, so nothing sits under the letters or down
the gaps between strokes. The violet stands about 6 mm off above and 1.5 to 2.5 mm below. The letters got heavier, and
any piece of line under 5 mm was removed.

**Weak spots.** The orange over "again" still wiggles with the letters' tops. The violet has a small break at the lower
left (kept, it reads as a hand lifting). The dots sit close to the n.
