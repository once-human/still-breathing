# 03 the seal (i am a party, front)

From fronts/all/15, made to read from across a room. The owner liked 15 but its words were too small and thin to see
from far away.

- The ring is now a solid cream band, 2.45 cm deep, with the words cut out of it: the shirt shows through the letters,
  so they read as big dark shapes on cream rather than thin cream lines on black.
- again.. runs level across the top and gets the most of the band (x-height about 1 cm); पुन्हा.. sits upright along
  the bottom; फिर से.. runs down the right and ಮತ್ತೆ.. up the left. Each word's two dots are orange. Round holes
  punched through the band separate the words.
- Inside, one warm body seen in heat, much bigger than in 15: violet, orange, pale yellow, a cream core set a little
  high, the back's own colours.
- Centre chest, 13.6 cm across, four inks (cream, orange, violet, pale yellow).

Weak spots: ಮತ್ತೆ is the smallest word (x-height about 0.8 cm) and, like फिर से.., reads sideways. Latin is Shantell
(the back titles' font) at a heavier weight; the Devanagari and Kannada are Noto Black.
