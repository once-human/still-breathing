# 04 the seal (i am a party, front)

Redrawn in one pass from fronts/all/15 (the draft he picked). Centre chest, 21 cm across. Four inks: cream, orange,
violet and pale yellow.

- **The ring is 15's.** Two hand-drawn lines, never filled in. Each circle is drawn in two goes, so the two arcs overlap
  and taper into each other, and the radius wanders slowly the way a hand's does. A cream dot sits in each gap
  between the halves.
- **The words, made to carry across a room.** again.. फिर से.. over the top, with their tops to the rim. पुन्हा..
  ಮತ್ತೆ.. upright along the bottom, about a fifth bigger, since they ride the longer outer part of the band. Each
  word's two dots are orange.
  - again.. is set in the back title's hand (Shantell) at its heaviest; the scripts are Noto Black. Weight is what
    carries, so the letters are nearly as tall as the band (again.. has an x-height of about 1.45 cm, against
    about 0.6 cm in 15).
  - Each letter or cluster is set a little off the line and off the upright. The whole word is bent round the ring
    with a slow wander, and the corners are softened the way ink softens them on rubber.
- **The heat body.** About 03's size: violet, orange, pale yellow and a cream core, set a little high. Each colour is
  painted on with a round brush going round, so the fill is made of the strokes: their tails flick out at each
  colour's edge, and long gaps show where one stroke ran past the next. Pinholes under 1 cm are filled, so there are
  no specks.
- **Print.** Binary alpha. Nothing under about 1 mm (check.py verify: 0 px thinner than the minimum, margins ok).
