# 02 section

**The idea.** The back is the plan; this is the section. A cut along Tumakuru Road at Madavara, drawn like an
architect's section by a printmaker: the last Green Line train lit on the viaduct, the concourse under it lit and full
of people all walking one way, out toward the grounds; the jam standing still on the road beneath (cars nose to tail,
some dark, some pale, a bus, a two-wheeler two up, one auto); a rain tree over the footpath; and under the road the cut
earth, the piers' footings and piles, a storm drain.

**Placement.** Centre chest, 27 cm wide, top 12.5 cm under the shoulder point, centred.

**Pairs with the back.** Same place, the other projection: the back looks down on it, the front cuts through it. The
back's notes ("the green line ends here", "everyone's on tumakuru road") become things you can see from the side.

**Medium.** Printmaker line with flat spot fills. Inks: the back's key and green, plus one yellow that does all the
light (train windows, the concourse, headlights, brake lights) and is the auto's own yellow. No words.

**Made.** Three draw rounds, one critic pass, one rework. From the critic: lower placement, a readable auto with room
round it and a solid hood, varied traffic bumper to bumper, the concourse broken into lit panes with bigger people and
an open end, a deeper cut with longer piles and a drain, cleaner tree edges. Not taken: yellow-roofed cabs (Bengaluru
cabs are white) and a much bigger auto (it must stay a detail). The sound rings were tried in three ways and cut: in
the thin strip of sky over the roof they always read as rain.

**Honest weak spots.**
- The scale is cheated: the people in the concourse are drawn larger than true so they survive the 1 mm minimum.
- Madavara's real station is drawn as a generic elevated station (canopy, platform, concourse on piers); its exact
  structure and the stair to BIEC are not shown.
- The two-wheeler is small and reads only up close.
