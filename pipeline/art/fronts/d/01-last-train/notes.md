# 01 last train

**The idea.** The moment before the back: the last Green Line train in at Madavara, the end of the line. Side-on
along the viaduct, the cab's nose stopped at the buffer stop, the station canopy over it, every car packed with people
standing against the lit windows. The drawing trails off to the left as if the artist stopped there.

**Placement.** Collarbone, wearer's left: 18.6 cm wide, top 12 cm under the shoulder point, centre 13 cm off centre.
A long thin object for a long thin place.

**Pairs with the back.** The back's note says "madavara. the green line ends here"; this is that train, close up,
before everyone walks to the grounds. Same key ink and the same green, used only for the Green Line's stripe and cab.

**Medium.** Ink line + one spot green. No words.

**Made.** Three draw rounds, one critic pass (placement too close to the collar, people too few and uniform, the
roof smeared with drips, buffer stop unreadable, uniform line weight, a weak fade), one rework acting on all of those
except a green sign plate.

**Honest weak spots.**
- Livery: drawn as a light body with the green stripe and green cab end. The rake type running on the Green Line in
  December 2026 (BEML or the new CRRC sets) was not confirmed, so the stripe is the line's colour, not a checked livery.
- The passengers are heads and shoulders only: at this size nothing finer survives the 1 mm minimum.
- The far car is only a few lines; some may read it as unfinished rather than fading.
