# 04 map, bengaluru: front brainstorm

The back is the plan: the NICE grounds from straight above in a printmaker's hand (key #22201D, green #2F7D52, lake
blue #6E95CE, sound blue #2C46BE), every person a head and two shoulders, friends holding hands, the last train in at
Madavara, Tumakuru Road jammed, the sound rings rolling out over the village to the lake. The fronts are written in
the same language: the map's line, its inks, its density. The shirt is the paper.

Who wears it: mostly people who came to Bengaluru for the show, international, sophisticated. They love the city's
night: its lights, the cool December air (16° after midnight), autos (green and yellow, black hood) and cabs (white,
yellow plates), the metro. No rangoli, jasmine, bus boards, landmark clip-art, slogans, coordinates or tracklists.

Facts checked for the drawing: Madavara is the end of the Green Line, an elevated station on the viaduct over the
Tumakuru Road (NH 48) median, so the last train stops at a buffer. The BIEC halls and the open ground sit just
north-east of it. Anchepalya lake lies south past the village. On 12 and 13 Dec 2026 the moon is a three-to-four-day
crescent that sets around nine, so after the show there is no moon.

## Ideas, by placement (17)

**Left chest (8-11 cm)**
1. **The map's key.** A printmaker's legend block lifted from the back: little swatches of its marks (hatched hall,
   a person, two holding hands, a sound arc, lake waves, the jam, the train) with lowercase hand labels, a north
   arrow and a scale bar, "16° after midnight" as the weather line, again.. as the cartouche title. The back's own
   furniture, worn on the chest.
2. **Two hands held.** Two hands clasped, drawn big in the map's ink, the only blue a broken sound arc passing over
   them. The back says "strangers, holding hands"; this is that, close up.
3. **The stage from above, rings out.** A small crop of the back's gesture: the stage block, the front rows, three
   broken blue rings.
4. **An auto at the gate.** Green and yellow, black hood, the driver asleep across the back seat. (Rejected as a
   whole piece: the auto must stay a detail.)

**Collarbone (12-20 cm)**
5. **The last train in at Madavara.** The Green Line train side-on along the viaduct, its nose at the buffer stop
   where the line ends, the platform canopy over it, people already on the stairs. A long thin object for a long
   thin place.
6. **The skyline of the halls.** The BIEC halls' long roofs in elevation, the stage's scaffold roof behind them.
   (Weaker: flat, a building portrait.)

**Centre chest (20-28 cm)**
7. **The section.** The back is the plan; the front is the section: a cut through Tumakuru Road at Madavara, drawn
   like an architect's section by a printmaker. The last train on the viaduct, the footbridge with people
   crossing, the jam underneath (cars, a white cab, an auto, a bike), a rain tree by the service road, the
   compound wall and the halls behind, the sound rings arriving from the right.
8. **The gate under the rain tree.** The crowd queuing into the grounds under one big rain tree, a streetlight, an
   auto waiting. (Strong, but the section does more and carries the same things.)

**Full front (up to 35 x 45 cm)**
9. **The crowd, zoomed.** The back's crowd at ten times the scale: still from straight above, the same people now
   drawn properly (hair, shoulders, raised arms, a jacket tied round a waist, a phone held up), friends in chains
   holding hands, the barricade at the top, the grass showing between them, the sound rings crossing over in blue.
   Find yourself in it.
10. **The ride.** The Green Line as a hand map from the city to Madavara. (Rejected: a metro-line diagram was
    already thrown out as AI-ish.)

**Low hem (10-30 cm)**
11. **Tumakuru Road, standing still.** A linocut band along the hem: the night cut out of a dark block, the jam as
    carved headlight strokes and red tail-light strokes doubled on the road, the viaduct over it with the last
    train's lit windows. The lights he loved as painterly strokes.
12. **The lake.** A hem corner: Anchepalya lake under the moonless night, wave hatching, the last broken sound rings
    arriving across the water.

**Vertical strip (5-8 cm x up to 40 cm)**
13. **The walk.** The real route from the Madavara platform to the ground, run top to bottom as one hand-drawn line
    in the sound blue, the walls, halls and roads around it drawn from the same map geometry and thinning out,
    people walking it in twos and threes.
14. **The rings, stacked.** The sound rings as a column of broken arcs. (Rejected: alone, it is a pattern.)

**Split**
15. **Where the sound starts, where it ends.** A small chest mark (the stage from above, the first rings) and a hem
    corner on the other side (the lake, the last rings reaching the water): the two ends of the back's one gesture,
    answering each other across the body.
16. **Train and jam.** The train at the collarbone, the jam at the hem. (Two good pieces, but they are versions on
    their own already.)

**Words**
17. **A note on the map.** One of the back's handwritten notes with its arrow ("we'll be somewhere in here") on the
    chest pointing at nothing. (Rejected: a joke that wears out.)

Why these: each is a different relation to the back. A detail lifted out (07 hands), a zoom (03 crowd), the other
projection (02 section), a moment before (01 the train arriving, 05 the walk in), what is outside it (04 the jam),
the map's own furniture (06 the key) and the back's one gesture split in two (08). Sizes run from a 9 cm mark to a
full front; six placements; four mediums. again.. appears once, as the legend's title; seven of the eight carry no
words. The auto is in two pieces and is the hero of none.
