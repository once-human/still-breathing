# 01 the key

## The idea
A map's key, the block a printed sheet carries in its corner, lifted off the back as an object of its own and titled
with the wordmark the way a survey sheet's key carries its publisher's name: **fred again..**, and under it
*mumbai, 8 + 9 dec*. Continued from the first-round draft he liked ("keep working on that").

Each swatch sits in its own hand-ruled cell, as on a printed legend, clipped to it, and is drawn in the back's own marks:
- **the city**: two blocks, hatched and drawn edge by edge, as the back draws every block.
- **the course**: a bend of the track, the turf in green inside the back's black double rail, a few grass marks cut out.
- **the sea**: three rows of the back's short wave strokes, in its sea blue.
- **us**: heads from above, packed at one side and thinning out, as the back draws the crowd.
- **no moon**: a pencilled circle left empty. Both Mumbai nights are moonless (new moon falls on 9 December 2026; on
  the 8th the moon is under 1% lit and below the horizon all evening). It replaces the first draft's "meet here" tent,
  the weakest swatch.
- **the sound**: the stage, a small blue block, and its rings swept one way and broken, cut by the cell the way the
  back's rings run off the sheet.

A ruled block at the foot holds the north arrow, a split dart with one half filled, turned as the back is turned
(north points right, a little up), and a survey scale bar with alternate quarters filled, true to the back: 125 m is
31.75 mm on the 38 cm back print.

All the small lettering (subtitle, labels, n, 125 m) is one face, Shantell Sans at 8 mm, the smallest size whose strokes
and the e's bar hold through the 1 mm clean-up; the wordmark is Bricolage, the lineup's face.

After the critic: labels cut to about 70% and moved to one face, ruled cells, smaller city blocks, the sound redrawn
without the speaker-icon look, the foot given its own ruled block and a map north arrow.

## Placement
Left chest: 10 cm wide, 16 cm tall, top 15 cm under the high shoulder point, inner edge 4 cm off centre.

## How it goes with the back
It is the back's legend: it explains the back without showing it. Every swatch is a mark from the back, so the two
sides read as one printed sheet, front and verso.

## Honest weak spots
- 16 cm tall is a tall panel for a left chest; it reads as a pocket-sized card rather than a small mark.
- The labels are no longer the back's note hand (Gochi Hand): at the size the critic asked for its strokes fall under
  1 mm, so they are Shantell Sans, one of the lineup's faces.
- The hatched city blocks are as fine as 1 mm lines allow at this size; they read heavier than the back's.

## Make it
`python3 gen.py` (print size: art.png and preview.png) or `R=1 python3 gen.py` (draft.png). Helpers in `../lib/`.
Inks: the back's own (black line #22201D, green #2F7D52, sea blue #6890C8, ring blue #2C46BE).
