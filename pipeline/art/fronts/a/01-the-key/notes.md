# 01 the key

## The idea
A map's key, the block a printed sheet carries in its corner, lifted off the back as its own object and titled with
the wordmark, the way a survey sheet's key carries its publisher's name. Continued from the first-round draft he liked
("keep working on that"): the same rows in the back's own marks (the city hatched, the course's turf and rails, the
sea's wave strokes, us as heads, the sound-desk tent as the meeting point, the rings), the north arrow turned the way
the back is turned (north points right), and a scale bar true to the back (125 m).

## Placement
Left chest, 10 cm wide, 15.6 cm tall, top 15 cm under the high shoulder point, inner edge 4 cm off centre.

## How it goes with the back
It is the back's legend: it explains the back without showing it. Every swatch is a mark from the back, so the two sides read as one printed map, front and verso.

## Honest weak spots
- 15.6 cm is tall for a left chest; it sits closer to a pocket-sized panel than a small mark.
- The labels are the back's note hand (Gochi Hand), slightly emboldened so its tapers survive the 1 mm clean-up.
- The crowd swatch ("us") is dots, as the back draws the crowd; they are 2 mm, well over the print minimum.

## Make it
`python3 gen.py` (print size, writes art.png and preview.png) or `R=1 python3 gen.py` (draft.png). Helpers in `../lib/`.
Inks: the back's own (black line #22201D, green #2F7D52, sea blue #6890C8, ring blue #2C46BE).
