# 02 looking up

## The idea
The back looks down on the course; this one lies on the infield and looks up, east, at 10 pm on 8 December 2026,
the real sky (positions computed for Mahalaxmi): Orion about 40 degrees up, Aldebaran and three of the Pleiades above,
Sirius low over the towers, Procyon. Two altitude circles as broken pale-blue arcs, the constellation figures in
ring-blue dashes, the towers of Lower Parel and the rain trees round the course leaning in toward the zenith, as a
fish-eye does. Continued from the first-round draft he liked ("just on the chest"), cut back for the chest: no stage
roof, fewer towers, only the stars a city sky shows, and the name set along the horizon's curve under the trees.

## Placement
Centre chest, 17 cm wide, top 11 cm under the high shoulder point.

## How it goes with the back
Same pen, same three inks, same place, opposite direction: the back is the map from above, the front is the sky from the ground. The rain trees are the course's own.

## Honest weak spots
- The biggest of the eight; still the most illustrative front.
- The tree crowns are now separate blobs, thinning toward both ends; at chest distance they read as a hedge, not trees one by one.

## Make it

Inks: the back's own (black line #22201D, green #2F7D52, sea blue #6890C8, ring blue #2C46BE).
