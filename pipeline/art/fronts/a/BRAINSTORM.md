# 03 map, mumbai: fronts brainstorm

The back is the Mahalaxmi race course from above, drawn like a printmaker's map: hatched blocks for the city, the
turf in green, the sea's short wave lines in pale blue round Haji Ali, the crowd as packed heads, and the sound rolling
out from the stage in broken deep-blue rings. Off-white shirt, so the shirt is the paper; inks are the back's own
(black line, green, sea blue, ring blue).

What the front has to do (BRIEF.md, his words): its own design, smaller and quieter than the back, a wordmark in nearly
every one (again.., fred again.., actual life, or again.. in four scripts), never the back's picture. The map key and
looking up were liked and carry on. Everything shown as the graphic alone on a patch of off white.

The front can borrow the back's **language** (map conventions, the pen, the inks) without borrowing its **picture**
(the course, the crowd, Haji Ali). That is the line every idea below is tested against.

## Ideas (19)

1. **The key** (liked): the map's legend block on its own, now with the wordmark as its title. Continue.
2. **Looking up** (liked): lying on the infield looking up at the real 10 pm sky over the towers. Continue, smaller.
3. **The sound**: again.. set heavy, the two dots in ring blue, three broken rings rolling out from them. The back's
   sound, with no course under it.
4. **Four in a line**: again.. / फिर से.. / पुन्हा.. / ಮತ್ತೆ.. in one line across the collarbone, the dots in blue.
   The four scripts, but never the old stacked block.
5. **Four in a ring**: the four scripts round a broken ring like a seal. (Close to 3's rings; dropped for 4.)
6. **fred again.. by hand**: the name lettered with the pen itself, letter by letter, with one line of the back's sea
   under it. The wordmark alone, made with care.
7. **Two dark nights**: again.. where the two dots are the two moonless show nights (new moon falls on 9 Dec 2026;
   on the 8th the moon is 0.6% lit and below the horizon all evening), each footnoted with its date.
8. **The neatline**: the corner of a survey sheet's graduated border, with its coordinates and the wordmark in the
   corner. Small, at the hem.
9. **The sea, recorded**: a voice note's waveform drawn by hand (Fred's voice notes), its swell the sea's, over
   actual life.
10. **The crossing**: two streets drawn as map casings, labelled actual life and fred again.. where they cross.
11. **Survey stamp**: a rectangular surveyor's ink stamp, coordinates and the wordmark. (Too close to 8.)
12. **Tide line**: again.. over the tide's curve; the walkway to Haji Ali opens and closes again and again. (Needs
    real tide numbers I cannot check offline; dropped.)
13. **North points right**: the back is turned so north points right; a big hand-drawn north arrow with again..
    (Lives inside the key instead.)
14. **Drawn twice**: again.. written twice, once green, once black, a little off, the second pass. (Reads as a
    misprint from across a room; dropped.)
15. **Scale bar**: a scale bar labelled 0 / again.. / again..; a joke more than a design. Dropped.
16. **Furlong post**: the course's furlong marker with the wordmark. Leans on the course, the back's subject. Dropped.
17. **Colour proof strip**: the three inks as hand-cut swatches beside the wordmark, a printer's proof. Generic.
18. **Phone frame**: filming the night; that idea belongs to the crowd shirt. Dropped here.
19. **Soundings**: depth numbers in the bay; needs invented numbers. Dropped.

## The eight, and why

The crossing (10) was the ninth; it lost to the waveform, which carries "actual life" with more feeling and less joke.

## Change: two fronts, not eight

The owner cut this shirt to two fronts, the artist's choice, made as well as possible. The six others (03 to 08) were
drafted and dropped before committing. The two kept are the two he liked, pushed much further, because both already
carry what the brief asks for (their own design, the back's language without its picture, room for a wordmark):

- **01 the key**, titled fred again.., with a true fact moved in from idea 7: a "no moon" row (both nights moonless).
- **02 looking up**, the real 10 pm sky, the name and a chart caption set along the horizon's curve.
