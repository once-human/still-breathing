# 03 map, mumbai: fronts

The owner cut this shirt to two fronts. Each went through six to eight draw, look, fix passes, one fresh critic and one
rework.

1. **01 the key**: the back map's legend as its own object, titled fred again.. (mumbai, 8 + 9 dec), six ruled swatches in the back's marks including "no moon", a north dart and a true scale. Left chest, 10 cm.
2. **02 looking up**: the real 10 pm sky over Mahalaxmi on 8 Dec (Orion, Sirius over the towers, the rain trees in a fish-eye bowl), fred again.. and "8 dec, 10 pm" along the horizon's curve. Centre chest, 17 cm.
